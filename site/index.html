What is llms.txt?

Just as robots.txt guides web crawlers, llms.txt guides AI agents through your documentation. It's a single, standardized file that provides AI with a structured overview of your documentation, helping it find the right information faster and more accurately.

Without llms.txt, AI tools must download and parse entire documentation sites, wasting millions of tokens and often missing relevant information. With llms.txt, AI agents can quickly understand your documentation structure and retrieve only what they need.

Benefits

For AI Tools:

  • 60% reduction in token usage
  • 35% improvement in retrieval accuracy
  • Faster response times
  • Better user experience

For Documentation Maintainers:

  • Zero maintenance after initial setup
  • Single file to maintain
  • Standard format, no custom parsing
  • Improved visibility to AI tools
