### Why Multiple N Values?

Validates scaling law:
- N=100: Noisy but fast (5 runs)
- N=1,000: Good signal-to-noise
- N=5,000: Demonstrates scaling
- N=10,000: Proves linear behavior
- N=20,000: Extrapolates to production scale
